Our Founding Story

Built out of
frustration — and ambition

In late 2021, Arjun Mehta was head of growth at a Series B startup, juggling five different browser tabs just to understand why their homepage had slipped from position 3 to position 11 overnight. "Every tool I tried was either too complex, too expensive, or built for agencies — not for teams like ours," he recalls.

He called his college friend Priya Sharma, who was leading infrastructure at a machine-learning company. Together, over a long weekend in a tiny Palo Alto kitchen, they sketched out what a truly team-first SEO platform would look like: real-time ranking data, collaborative workflows, actionable insights surfaced automatically — no PhD in data science required.

By March 2022, Rankly's first prototype was live. The early waitlist filled in 48 hours. By summer, they had raised a seed round and assembled a team of eight. By year-end, over 1,000 companies had replaced their patchwork of tools with Rankly.

Today Rankly tracks billions of keywords across 190+ countries, powers growth for everyone from bootstrapped indie makers to Fortune 500 marketing departments, and remains obsessively focused on one thing: helping every team earn the organic traffic they deserve.

"The best SEO tool isn't the one with the most features — it's the one your whole team actually uses every day. We built Rankly to be that tool."

What We Stand For

Our core values

These aren't poster words — they shape every product decision, hiring call, and customer interaction.

🔍

Radical Clarity

Search data is inherently complex. Our job is to strip away the noise and surface exactly what matters. We say no to vanity metrics and yes to signal over noise.

🤝

Teams First

SEO is a team sport. Every feature we ship is designed for collaboration — sharing insights, assigning tasks, celebrating wins together. Solo dashboards are so 2015.

⚡

Relentless Speed

Rankings change by the hour. Decisions can't wait. We invest deeply in infrastructure so our data is always fresh, our UI is always fast, and our customers always feel ahead.

🌱

Long-Game Thinking

Organic growth compounds. So does trust. We build for durability — in our product, our relationships with customers, and in the sustainable business we're creating together.
